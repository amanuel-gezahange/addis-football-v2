Privacy Policy & Support

Addis Football helps players discover and join pickup football games in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Addis Football handles information when you use the Addis Football mobile application.

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Information We Collect

We may collect information necessary to provide and operate the service, including:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your account or user identifier
  • Your football game bookings and purchase history

How We Use Information

We use this information to provide app functionality, including creating and maintaining accounts, authenticating users, displaying football games, managing bookings, processing game participation, providing customer support, and maintaining the security and reliability of the service.

Payments

Paid game bookings may use third-party payment providers such as Chapa and Telebirr. Addis Football does not store your payment card, bank account, or Telebirr credentials. Payment providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Service Providers

Addis Football may use third-party service providers to provide authentication, database hosting, payment processing, messaging, infrastructure, and other functionality necessary to operate the service.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the app, process payments, provide authentication, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, or protect the service and its users.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain transaction and booking records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and protect the security of the service.

Your Choices

You may contact Addis Football if you have questions about your personal information or would like to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.

Children's Privacy

Addis Football is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service or our privacy practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
