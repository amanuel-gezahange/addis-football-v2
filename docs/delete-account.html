Delete Your Addis Football Account

Addis Football users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

Send an email to the support email address listed on the Addis Football Google Play Store listing. Use the subject "Delete My Addis Football Account" and include the phone number associated with your account.

What will be deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete or anonymize personal information associated with the account, including profile information and account identifiers, where applicable.

Data that may be retained

Certain transaction, payment, security, fraud-prevention, or other records may be retained where required for legitimate business, regulatory, accounting, dispute-resolution, or legal purposes.

Data that is not required to be retained will be deleted or anonymized after the deletion request is processed.

Contact

For questions about account deletion, contact us using the support email address shown on the Addis Football Google Play Store listing.
